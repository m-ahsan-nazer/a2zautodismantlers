## Your contact information

When you contact us using your contact details in our contact form we use that information to verify ownership of vehicle. None of your information is retained for future business use. We don’t pass your data to other companies and we do not use your data for future advertising.

As a licensed second hand vehicle dealer we are required to maintain records of vehicle we buy by law. An enquiry that results in sale of a vehicle to us, requires us to ensure that the vehicle is not stolen. We are dedicated to protecting our customers privacy and data and ensuring we follow the law.
