Not every car that [Christchurch wreckers](/) like us buy has a simple story. Plenty of the enquiries we get are about cars that can't be driven, cars that have been through something, or cars that are in the wrong place at the wrong time.

Here are the situations we see most often, and what you need to know about each before you sell.

## The car won't start, or it's stuck where it is

"It's been sitting in the garage and needs to be pushed out." "It's stuck in a driveway." "The starter won't even click." We hear this every week.

A car that won't start is not a problem for a wrecker. Our tow trucks collect non-running vehicles, and [removal is free](/scrap-car-removal/) across Christchurch and Canterbury. To make pickup smooth:

- **Tell us about access.** Is it in a garage, down a narrow shared driveway, on the lawn, or blocked in by another car? Flat tyres or a locked steering wheel are worth mentioning too.
- **Have the keys ready** if you have them, even if the battery is dead.
- **Don't buy a new battery just to sell it.** A Nissan Elgrand we were asked about needed a battery and had a flat tyre. Neither changes much for a wrecker.

## The car was stolen, vandalised or broken into

Several people contact us each month after their car has been stolen and recovered. Typical damage includes smashed windows, a broken ignition barrel, a trashed interior or a car left on the side of the road with a flat tyre. Sometimes the car ends up at a towing yard, and storage fees start adding up quickly.

Things to know:

- **Ownership must be clear.** As a licensed second-hand vehicle dealer, we are required to keep records of the vehicles we buy and make sure they are not stolen. Have your ID and proof of ownership ready.
- **Tell us where the car is now.** If it's at a towing or storage yard, let us know which one so we can plan the collection.
- **Ignition damage and missing keys are common.** Mention it when you ask for a quote so we bring the right gear.

## It was in a crash and insurance won't cover it

Some of the hardest stories we hear are crashes without valid insurance, like a car crashed by an unlicensed driver or a parked car hit by a stolen vehicle. Others are insurance write-offs where the owner is left with the wreck.

Accident damage, even chassis damage, doesn't stop us buying a car. A 2001 Toyota Hilux rear-ended hard enough to twist the chassis, and a 2012 Toyota written off after a rollover, are both cars we were asked to quote on. Photos of the damage help us give you an accurate price.

## No WOF, no rego, or deregistered

Cars that have been off the road for a while are some of the easiest to sell to a wrecker. We regularly buy cars with:

- an expired WOF that would need tyres, shocks or struts to pass
- rego on hold or expired
- registration cancelled (deregistered), sometimes for years

A deregistered car can't legally be driven on the road, so it will need to be towed. We do that for free. Whatever the status of the car, you still need to tell NZTA you've sold it. Our [selling car NZ checklist](/2025/06/01/selling-car-nz-checklist/) links to the NZTA Notice of Disposal and the official guide on [selling your vehicle to a wrecker](https://www.nzta.govt.nz/vehicles/not-using-your-vehicle/selling-your-vehicle-to-a-wrecker/).

## The car is at a mechanic's, or out of town

A lot of cars we buy are not at the owner's house. They are sitting at a workshop after a bad diagnosis, at a friend's place, or stranded somewhere on a trip. That includes a car with a stuck thermostat at Castle Hill, one with a blown head gasket near Arthurs Pass, and utes in Ashburton and Timaru.

- **Cars at a workshop:** let the workshop know it will be collected, and settle any bill you owe them first.
- **Out-of-town vehicles:** we cover Christchurch, Selwyn and Waimakariri, and for trucks, vans and 4WDs we travel further into wider Canterbury and Otago. Tell us exactly where the vehicle is and we'll let you know what we can do.

## You're leaving the country, or already have

Moving overseas is a common reason to sell in a hurry. If you can, sort the sale out before you leave: get a quote, book a pickup date and finish the paperwork while you're still here.

If you have already gone and the car is with a friend, contact us by email and explain the situation. We still need to confirm ownership, so be ready to provide your details and arrange for someone to hand over the keys.

## What we can't help with

- **Financed vehicles.** We don't buy cars that still have money owing on them. Pay off the loan first.
- **Car parts.** We get asked for these a lot, but we don't sell car parts. Our [contact form](/contact-us/) is only for people who want to sell a vehicle.
