Get paid more when you sell your vehicle and save yourself time with this helpful list of must knows from the team at A2Z Auto Dismantlers.

## 1. What kind of payout can I expect for my wrecked vehicle?

Your payout depends on factors like vehicle type, condition, and engine size. Vans and trucks usually earn more due to their higher scrap value.

## 2. Will I need to deliver the car myself?

No need! A2Z offers [free car removal](/scrap-car-removal/) across Christchurch. We’ll pick up your vehicle from your home or workplace at a time that suits you.

## 3. What documents or details should I prepare before selling?

- Your driver licence
- Bank account number for payment not in cash
- Any spare keys

## 4. Can I sell my car quickly without going through auctions?

Yes. We offer [cash for cars](/cash-for-cars/) with a direct sale, no auctions needed. Book a time, bring in your vehicle (or let us pick it up), and get an offer right away.

## 5. Are there any hidden costs or seller fees?

If you sell directly to A2Z, there are no seller fees. If you were selling through an online auction site then yes.

## 6. How soon will I receive payment after selling my car?

Cash payment is on pickup. For bank transfers, usually on the same day the vehicle is picked up and ownership is confirmed.

## 7. Can I still sell a car that’s not working?

Yes, we regularly buy non-running, wrecked, or undrivable vehicles. Whether it’s damaged, has mechanical issues, or is just old, we’ll take it.

## 8. Will you buy my car if it’s deregistered or lacks a WoF?

Absolutely. No WoF or registration? No problem. We buy all vehicles, even those off the road or deregistered.

## 9. Is pickup time flexible and reliable?

Yes, we work around your schedule and always aim to be on time. If there’s ever a delay, we’ll keep you informed in advance.

## 10. Can I sell my financed vehicle to wreckers?

No. When we buy a vehicle we ensure that is not financed or stolen.
